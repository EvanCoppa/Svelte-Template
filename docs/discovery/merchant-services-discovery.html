Discovery questionnaire

Tell us how you run your shop

We are building your platform around how your office actually works — your funnel, your words, your residual process — rather than bending your business to fit generic CRM. These questions are how we find out. Your answers decide what we turn on for you on day one, and what we build next.

Who should fill this in
Whoever runs the sales floor, plus whoever reconciles residuals. Those are usually two people, and the second half of this matters most.
How long it takes
About 25 minutes. Skip anything that does not apply to you — a blank is an answer.
What happens next
We turn your answers into a build plan and walk you through it: what ships first, what comes later, and what we would not build at all.
Two notes before you start.
  • Where we have offered checkboxes, tick as many as are true — and write in the margin when none of them is quite right. The write-ins are the most useful thing in here.
  • Do not tidy up your answers. If the honest answer is "a spreadsheet nobody but Karen understands", that is exactly what we need to see.

Your business

The shape of the company, so we size everything else correctly.

  • Registered ISO / MSP

  • Sub-ISO

  • Something else (please say)
Q2
Which processors or back-ends do you board merchants to?
List all of them, even the one you only use twice a year. Each one is a different report format and a different set of rules on our side.
Q3

Ballpark is fine. We are asking because forty reps and four reps are genuinely different products.

04

Getting a merchant live

Everything between a signature and a first batch.

Q14
What paperwork does an application need, and where does it live today while you are collecting it?
Signed app, statements, voided check, ID, business licence — and honestly: email inbox, a shared drive, a filing cabinet?
Q15
After a merchant is live, what do they call you about, and who picks up the phone?
Chargebacks, a deposit that did not land, a dead terminal, a fee they do not recognise — and what you call that queue internally.
Q16
Would you ever want merchants themselves to log in — to see statements, open a ticket, or check a deposit?
  • Yes, that would be valuable
  • Maybe later
  • No — we would rather they call us
Q17
Who inside your office should not be able to see something?
This is the most important question on the page. For example: should a rep be able to see another rep's merchants? Their residuals? Should a support person see commission splits? Tell us what would cause a problem if the wrong person saw it.
05

Residuals and getting paid

This is the part we most want to get right, because it is the part that happens every month forever. Please be as specific as you can stand to be.

If you only answer one section, make it this one. Everything else in this document is about winning a merchant. This section is about the money that arrives afterwards, which is the actual business — and it is where software usually helps the most.
Q18
How do residuals reach you each month, and in what format?
A CSV you download from a portal, a spreadsheet emailed to you, a PDF, a report you log in to read on screen? And roughly what day of the month?
Q19
How many separate reports do you reconcile each month, and do they agree with each other?
Q20
How are splits decided, and how often do they change?
A flat percentage per rep? Per merchant? Tiered on volume? Does a rep's split change when they hit a number, or when an account is handed over?
Q21
Describe what actually happens today, step by step, from the report landing to the reps being paid.
Who does it, in what tool, and how many hours does it take? If it is a spreadsheet, we would love a copy with the names blanked out — that one file would tell us more than this whole page.
Q22
Do your reps get a statement showing how their number was arrived at? Do they trust it?
And how often do they come back and argue with it? We are trying to work out whether a self-serve rep statement is worth building early.

07

Sensitive data

A short section, but we would rather ask than assume.

Q26
What sensitive information would you want stored in the system, and what should never touch it?
Applications contain Social Security numbers, tax IDs and bank details. Some offices want those held; others deliberately keep them only in the processor's portal. Card numbers we will not store under any circumstances. Tell us where your line is and we will design to it.

What you look at

Q27
What numbers do you check every week, and what do you check every month?
New accounts boarded, volume, attrition, rep performance, residual per account — and which one, if it moved the wrong way, would ruin your week?
09

Where AI could help

We would rather build the two things you would use daily than ten you would not. Please rank only the ones you care about — 1 for the most useful — and leave the rest blank.

Q28
Rank what would genuinely save you time.
What it would do Rank
Read a competitor's statement for you
A rep photographs the merchant's current statement; the volume, transaction count, fees and effective rate come back filled in.
Draft the proposal from that statement
Two or three priced options against their current cost, ready for the rep to adjust and present.
Warn you when a merchant is slipping away
Flags accounts whose volume has dropped sharply, before they close — so someone can call while it still matters.
Check the processor's residual math
Compares what you were paid against what the accounts should have produced, and flags the gaps.
Brief a rep before a visit
One short summary of the merchant: history with you, open issues, what was quoted last time, what to watch for.
Write up a visit from the rep talking
The rep speaks for thirty seconds in the car; the notes, the follow-up tasks and the deal's new stage are recorded for them.
Pre-check an application before underwriting
Flags a business type or a detail likely to be declined, before the paperwork is done.
Answer the questions merchants repeat
Deposit timing, what a fee is, how a chargeback works — drafted for your support person, or answered directly.
Q29
What did you picture when you heard we were building AI into this?
Genuinely open. If you have seen something elsewhere that impressed you — or something that was sold as AI and turned out to be nothing — we would like to hear about both.
Q30
What should a computer never do on its own in your business?
Send something to a merchant unreviewed? Change a rate? Move a deal forward? Knowing your limits up front is how we avoid building something you would have to switch off.
